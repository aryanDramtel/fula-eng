# Pulaar-English-French Dictionary  
*(Fouta Djallon & Fouta Toro Dialects)*

This is a collection of Pulaar-English-French word translations, with a focus on the **Fouta Djallon** and **Fouta Toro** dialects spoken in Guinea and Senegal. Pulaar is a widely spoken language across West Africa, but each region has its own flavor — both in pronunciation and vocabulary. These two dialects are significantly different from others spoken in Mali, Nigeria, or Mauritania.

As many people from **Fouta Djallon** and **Fouta Toro** also speak **French**, this dictionary includes French translations alongside English wherever possible.

---

##  Why This Project?

Finding solid resources for either dialect has been frustratingly difficult. Most online references are either inaccurate, too generalized, or based on unrelated Pulaar variants. This dictionary was born out of a need for something more representative of how the language is truly spoken in these communities.

While we’ve done our best to ensure accuracy, Pulaar isn’t fully standardized across regions — so some entries may evolve as native speakers contribute.

---

##  How You Can Help

If you’re a native speaker or fluent in either dialect, your input would be invaluable! You can help by:
- Correcting existing translations
- Adding words or expressions unique to your region
- Suggesting alternate spellings or pronunciation notes
- Contributing French equivalents where they’re missing

Just open an issue or submit a pull request — every contribution brings us closer to a richer, truer linguistic resource.
